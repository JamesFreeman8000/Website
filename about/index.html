Soft Wash vs. Pressure — We Know the Difference

Not every surface should be hit with high pressure. Blast a roof or delicate siding and you can cause real damage. That's why we're trained in soft washing — using low pressure and professional cleaning agents to safely dissolve dirt and kill algae at the source — as well as traditional pressure washing for tough surfaces like concrete. Knowing which method to use, and when, is what separates a pro from a guy with a rented machine.
